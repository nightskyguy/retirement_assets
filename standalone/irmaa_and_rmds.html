IMPORTANT NOTE The IRMAA Thresholds and Penalties move at different rates, and that is what makes IRMAA worse over time. The income thresholds are indexed to CPI. The penalty is not: it is tied to Medicare, which grows faster. This tool carries thresholds forward at 3.2% a year, the ten-year average of the US consumer price index, and the penalty on the Medicare Trustees' own published path, which starts near 6.6% a year and eases toward 3.8% in the long run.
